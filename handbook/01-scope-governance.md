# Section 01: Scope and Governance

**Status:** Normative
**Edition:** 1.0 (2026)

---

## 1.1 Purpose

This handbook establishes binding data standards for all institutions under the jurisdiction of the Department of Education (DepEd), the Commission on Higher Education (CHED), and the Technical Education and Skills Development Authority (TESDA). It defines how data elements — school identifiers, location data, dates, student records, and related fields — shall be captured, stored, and exchanged across the Philippine education sector.

These standards draw from internationally recognized standards bodies including the International Organization for Standardization (ISO), the Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA), and established e-governance frameworks, adapted to the Philippine context.

---

## 1.2 Covered Agencies and Institutions

These standards apply to:

- All public and private basic education institutions under DepEd supervision (K–12)
- All higher education institutions (HEIs) under CHED jurisdiction
- All TESDA-registered technical-vocational institutions (TVIs) and assessment centers
- ICT systems and databases maintained by or on behalf of the above, including third-party vendors and service providers

---

## 1.3 Governing Standards Bodies Referenced

| Body | Relevance to This Handbook |
|------|---------------------------|
| ISO (International Organization for Standardization) | Core data format standards (dates, codes, country/language/currency) |
| Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA) | Geographic codes, civil registry conventions, PhilSys |
| Philippine Standard Geographic Code (PSGC) | Official barangay, municipality, city, province, and region codes |
| DICT (Dept. of Information and Communications Technology) | e-Government Interoperability Framework (eGIF) |
| ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 36 | IT for learning, education, and training (ITLET) standards |
| UNESCO | ISCED (education level classification) and ISCED-F (fields of education) |
| National Privacy Commission (NPC) | Data Privacy Act implementation, personal data handling |

---

## 1.4 Normative Language

This handbook uses the following conventions, consistent with ISO/IEC Directives Part 2:

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **shall** | The requirement is mandatory |
| **shall not** | The prohibition is mandatory |
| **should** | The requirement is recommended but not mandatory |
| **may** | The requirement is permitted but optional |
| **can** | A statement of possibility or capability |

---

## 1.5 Relationship to Existing Agency Systems

This handbook does not replace or supersede existing DepEd Orders, CHED Memorandum Orders (CMOs), or TESDA circulars. Where this handbook conflicts with a current agency issuance, the agency issuance prevails until formally reconciled through the Inter-Agency Data Governance Committee (IDGC) process.

Where agency issuances are silent on a data format or standard, this handbook shall apply.
